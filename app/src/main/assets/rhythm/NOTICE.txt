Prism includes BeatNet model 1 and model 3 weights, converted from PyTorch to ONNX.
The conversion exposes recurrent state explicitly and applies output softmax.
Prism supplies its own Kotlin audio frontend, tempo/phase/bar tracker, capture
integration, and visual response. It does not use BeatNet's particle filter.
Conversion scripts, original architecture, hashes, and validation are included
in the source project under tools/beatnet/. No upstream endorsement is implied.
